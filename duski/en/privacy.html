Duski Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Duski (The IT Crowd, Ed Cafferata) is built on one principle: all sound and all animations are generated 100% on your own device. No recordings are played, nothing is streamed, and there is no server listening or watching.

What data does Duski collect?

None. Duski does not collect, transmit or sell personal data, analytics or advertising identifiers. There is no tracking in the app.

What is stored locally?

Only your settings, such as the chosen age group (baby, child, teen, adult or senior), are kept on your device, so the app shows the right sounds and safety limits next time. This data never leaves your device.

Subscriptions (in-app purchases)

Duski Premium is handled by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). Payment details and purchase history are processed entirely through your Apple or Google account under their own privacy policies. Duski does not see or store payment details.

Children

Duski has dedicated, safer modes for babies and children (including a built-in volume limit), and does not collect personal data in those modes either.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
