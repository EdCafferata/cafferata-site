Duski Premium (auto-renewable subscription)

  • Plans: Duski Premium Monthly (1 month) and Duski Premium Yearly (1 year), both renewing automatically
  • Price: the exact amount for your country is shown in the app before purchase
  • Free trial: the first month is free for new subscribers

Payment is charged to your Apple or Google account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Renewal is charged within 24 hours before the end of the period. You manage and cancel your subscription in the account settings of the store. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

Health and safety

Duski is an aid for relaxation and sleep, not a medical device. With babies, always use baby mode with the built-in volume limit and do not place the device right next to the baby's head.
